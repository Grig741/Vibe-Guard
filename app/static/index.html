    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Is it safe to paste my URL or firebaseConfig?

    Yes. Your URL and the Firebase / Supabase config are already public — they ship inside your app's JavaScript for anyone to read. VibeGuard uses them only for the scan and never stores keys; only the project name, the grade and findings are saved.

    Will the scan change or break my data?

    No. By default every check is read-only. Only if you tick “This is my project” does VibeGuard also run active tests: a single __vibeguard_probe__ entry that is deleted immediately, and a throwaway sign-up that is removed. Your real data is never touched.

    Can I scan someone else's app?

    Only with permission. VibeGuard is built for checking projects you own or are authorized to test.

    What does the full report include?

    Every hole found, what an attacker can do with it, the exact request that got through, and step-by-step fixes with security rules you can paste straight into the Firebase or Supabase console. One purchase includes several full scans so you can re-check after fixing.

    How do I pay?

    On the website, buy a license key and paste it under your report. In Telegram, tap Unlock and pay with Stars — the key is sent to your chat automatically and works on the website too.

    Do you support Supabase?

    Yes. VibeGuard checks Supabase Row Level Security on your tables (public read/write), public storage buckets, an exposed database schema, and the single worst mistake — a service_role key shipped in your frontend. Paste your Supabase URL + anon key, or just give us your site URL and we'll find it.

    What else do you scan besides the database?

    When you scan by URL, VibeGuard also sweeps your shipped JavaScript for leaked API keys (OpenAI, Anthropic, Stripe, AWS, GitHub, SendGrid, database URLs and more), and checks for a public .env or .git, production source maps, and missing security headers.
